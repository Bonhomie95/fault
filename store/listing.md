## Description  (≤ 4000)  [~2540]

You have been summoned for jury duty.

The file lands in front of you. A defendant, a charge, three pieces of
evidence, two witnesses and two lawyers who both sound right. You have 120
seconds. Then the room wants an answer: GUILTY or NOT GUILTY.

FAULT is a game about judgement under pressure — and about living with it.

EVERY CASE IS DIFFERENT
Each case is written fresh for you and set in real places: the district
courts and police services of your own country. Every piece of evidence has
two honest readings. Every witness is hiding something. Some cases have a
right answer buried in the details. Some genuinely do not.

THE ROOM TALKS BACK
The accused pleads, argues, goes quiet. Witnesses dig in. Counsel needle each
other across the room. Turn on voices and hear every person in the courtroom
speak — or read it, at your own text size.

A CITY THAT REACTS
Your verdicts do not disappear. Convict too freely and trust in the courts
erodes. Acquit the wrong person and the streets notice. Crime, police
integrity, organised crime and media pressure all move with what you decide —
and the city's newspapers print the fallout: backlash, protests, reform bills,
a syndicate getting bolder.

PEOPLE COME BACK
The defendant you let walk may turn up again — as a witness, a victim, or in
the dock. The record remembers, and so does the city.

WHAT KIND OF JUROR ARE YOU?
FAULT quietly measures how you judge: how often you convict, how closely you
follow the evidence, and whether a defendant's face, manner or money sways
you. At each review you read a profile of yourself written like a newspaper
column. It is not always flattering.

CLIMB THE BENCH
Missions every day and week. Streaks for showing up. Rank up to unlock new
districts and cities to preside over, then apply to sit in courts beyond your
own. Compare your city with every other juror's on the boards — the most
peaceful, and the most lawless.

FAIR BY DESIGN
Nothing for sale changes a verdict, your standing, or a second on the clock.
Merit is earned by sitting cases, never by being right.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Play as a guest in seconds, or sign in with Apple to keep your career safe.
No adverts during a case, ever.
