# Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy

We respect intellectual property rights and expect players to do the same. This policy explains how to tell us that something in FAULT infringes your copyright or other rights, and how to respond if something you submitted was removed.

FAULT's case files are generated in part by artificial intelligence. If generated content reproduces your protected work, or uses your name or likeness, tell us and we will act quickly.

## Reporting infringement

Send a written notice to our designated agent:

**[AGENT NAME / EMAIL]**
[COMPANY NAME], [COMPANY ADDRESS]

Your notice must include:

- your physical or electronic signature;
- identification of the copyrighted work (or other right) you believe is infringed;
- identification of the material you believe is infringing, with enough detail for us to find it — for example, the juror name as it appears on the leaderboard, or the case title and case number and a screenshot;
- your contact information: name, address, telephone number and email;
- a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent, or the law;
- a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or are authorised to act on the owner's behalf.

For a generated case that you believe names or depicts a real person, you can also use the in-game **Report** button, choosing "This names a real person". A reported case is withdrawn from play immediately while it is reviewed.

## What we do

When we receive a valid notice, we will remove or disable the material promptly and, where the material came from a player (for example, a juror name), make reasonable efforts to tell that player.

## Counter-notice

If content you submitted was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the agent above that includes:

- your physical or electronic signature;
- identification of the material removed and where it appeared before removal;
- a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification;
- your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you are outside the United States, any judicial district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may restore the material in 10 to 14 business days unless the original complainant tells us they have filed a court action.

## Repeat infringers

We will terminate the accounts of players who repeatedly infringe the rights of others, in appropriate circumstances.

## Misrepresentation

Knowingly making a false claim in a notice or counter-notice may make you liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees.
